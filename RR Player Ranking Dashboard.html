Seven-Factor Player Ranking for Rajasthan Royals

A scouting-support model for T20 and T20I batters and bowlers, built for the RR Hackathon.

📋 Part 1: The Short Version

What this is: A ranking model that scores 145 batters and 140 bowlers on seven factors and combines them into one Final Score from 0 to 100. It is a shortlisting tool for scouts, not an automatic selection list.

How the Final Score is built: Every player is turned into a percentile (0 to 100) on each of seven factors. The factors are then blended using fixed weights. In short, more than half of the score (55.1%) comes from recent consistency (F1) and performance under pressure (F2).

🔬 Part 2: The Detailed Version

1. Dataset and Analysis Scope

The latest successful pipeline run produced 865 matches and 196,630 delivery rows, covering 145 eligible batters and 140 eligible bowlers between 22 January 2022 and 1 November 2026. Both T20 and T20I formats are included.

The uploaded delivery file contained 200,999 rows. The difference of 4,369 rows comes from the competition, date and innings filters. It does not mean all excluded rows were invalid deliveries.

2. Match-Level Scoring: The Foundation

Before any factor is calculated, every player's performance in every match receives a score between 0 and 100. All seven factors build on these match scores. They are analyst-defined scoring rubrics, not official cricket ratings.

3. Detailed Formulas for All 7 Factors
F1: Recent Consistency 28.50%
Window: 2 Nov 2025 to 1 Nov 2026. Evaluates scoring ≥60 in match model (P60), score stability (std dev), and role rates.
F1_base = 0.65 × P60 + 0.35 × P_stability
Batters: 0.60×F1_base + 0.20×P_SR + 0.10×P_Boundary + 0.10×P_InvDot
Bowlers: 0.60×F1_base + 0.20×P_InvEcon + 0.10×P_WktRate + 0.10×P_Dot
Why RR uses it: Identifies repeatable output rather than isolated 1-2 freak games.
F2: Pressure & Collapse 26.60%
Collapse: 4+ wickets lost, or ≥2 wickets lost in previous 18 balls. Pressure: collapse or 2nd innings RRR ≥ 9.
Batters: 0.50 × P_pressure_SR + 0.50 × P_collapse_SR
Bowlers: 0.35×P_InvPressEcon + 0.25×P_PressWkt + 0.25×P_InvCollEcon + 0.15×P_CollWkt
Why RR uses it: Pinpoints clutch players who rescue collapsed innings or defend under pressure.
F3: Match Participant Comparison 18.05%
Judges performance relative to all 22 players in the exact same match conditions.
Rank score = 100 × (1 - (Rank - 1) / 21)
Combines 60% within-match rank score and 40% proportion of performances beating the match mean.
Why RR uses it: Normalizes tough pitches (e.g. low-scoring turners or highway tracks).
F4: Opposition Strength 10.45%
Batters are judged against opposing bowling unit quality; bowlers against opposing batting unit quality. Only strictly earlier match dates are used (no future leakage).
Why RR uses it: Discounts padded stats recorded against weaker tier/associate attacks.
F5: Match-Winning Contribution 7.60%
F5_raw = mean score in wins - mean score in losses
Converted into a percentile. Players with 0 wins or 0 losses receive a neutral 50.
Why RR uses it: Identifies correlation between individual output and victory margin.
F6: Historical Performance 3.80%
Covers 22 Jan 2022 to 1 Nov 2025. Player's mean match score over the historical window converted into a percentile.
Why RR uses it: Retains long-term track record without drowning recent form.
F7: De-overlapped Overall Performance 5.00%
Residualized via OLS Regression to eliminate multicollinearity:
F7_base = b0 + b1×F2 + b2×F3 + b3×F6 + error
The residual errors are percentile-ranked, isolating purely orthogonal overall quality.
Why RR uses it: Prevents double-counting traits already rewarded in F2, F3, and F6.

5. How Rajasthan Royals Can Use the Results
1. Shortlisting

Find undervalued players across global leagues worth deeper video & live scouting.

2. Recent Form (F1)

Check 12-month rolling repeatability of performance before committing funds.

3. Pressure (F2)

Review player poise in top-order collapses and demanding second-innings chases.

4. Opposition Context (F4)

Understand the caliber of attacks faced to prevent inflation from secondary leagues.

5. Winning Impact (F5)

Compare performance in wins vs losses (association indicator, not causal proof).

6. Role Segmentation

Filter candidates by batting entry point (PP, Middle, Death) or bowling phase.

7. Auction Strategy

Blend ranking output with price valuation, overseas slot limits, and squad balance.

6. Critical Governance & Model Limitations
  • Analyst-Defined Rubrics: Match scores are proprietary rubrics tailored for T20 impact, not official ICC ratings.
  • Sample Size Caveats: Players with few matches can receive extreme percentile scores (e.g. Maaz Sadaqat's 8 matches vs Harry Brook's 55 matches). A high Final Score is a reason to scout a player, not to buy one.
  • Correlation vs Causation: F5 reflects statistical association with team victories, not definitive proof of individual cause.
  • External Factors: Player auction valuation, injury history, visa availability, and team chemistry sit outside the mathematical model.
Match scores are analyst-defined algorithmic rubrics, not official ICC ratings. Small match samples require qualitative scouting confirmation.
